About Ben Crossland

Explorer & Content Creator

My name is Ben Crossland, I'm 25 years old, and I have an unquenchable passion for exploring the great outdoors. My heart belongs to New Zealand, where every corner reveals a new story waiting to be captured.

For me, nature isn't just a backdrop—it's a way of life. Whether I'm hiking through native bush trails, standing at the edge of a quiet beach, or walking across rolling farmland, I find myself constantly inspired by the raw beauty of the environment around me.

My mission through Relaxation Station is simple: to bring these experiences to you. I believe that in our fast-paced world, we all deserve moments of genuine peace and connection with nature. Through my carefully crafted videos, I want to help you slow down, breathe deeply, and experience the healing power of the natural world—right from your own home.

The Birth of Relaxation Station

Relaxation Station began as a simple idea during the quietest hours of the evening. I'd spend countless nights exploring New Zealand's most serene locations—native bush trails where ancient trees whisper secrets, quiet beaches where waves create a natural rhythm, rolling farmland bathed in golden light, and peaceful countryside that feels untouched by time.

I realized that these moments of profound peace—moments where the only sounds you hear are birdsong, rivers flowing, rain gently falling, and wind rustling through leaves—were something the world needed more of.

So I made a commitment: to carefully plan, film, edit, and upload videos that capture real environments with authentic sounds and the natural atmosphere of each location. Every video is created with intention—not just to be pretty, but to actually help you.

Whether you're looking to study without distractions, relax after a demanding day, fall asleep naturally, or simply take a mental break from the chaos of modern life, Relaxation Station is your sanctuary. From calming walks and historical ambience to the gentle soundscapes of New Zealand's most beautiful landscapes, I've created a space where you can truly switch off and reconnect.

Our Vision Moving Forward

My plans for Relaxation Station are ambitious and heartfelt. I want to continue exploring every hidden gem that New Zealand has to offer—from the smallest forest streams to the grandest mountain vistas. I'm committed to expanding into different types of relaxing content: deeper dives into New Zealand's rich history, more specialized ASMR experiences, seasonal explorations, and collaborations with other creators who share this vision of mindful, authentic nature content.

But most importantly, I want to build a community of people who believe that slowing down is not laziness—it's necessity. A place where you can find refuge, inspiration, and genuine peace. Where nature becomes not just a video you watch, but a state of mind you carry with you.
